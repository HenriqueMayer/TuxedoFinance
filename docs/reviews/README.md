The [coverage baseline](../coverage-baseline.md) explains the measurement policy
and distinguishes historical measurements from the CI floor. The health review's
[performance artifact](project-health-2026-10-16-performance.json) is synthetic
local evidence, not an installation database or a latency guarantee.

New evidence should identify its date, code/revision scope, environment, commands,
outcome, inspected states and remaining limitations. Keep raw screenshots and
temporary logs out of the normal installation path. Update current guides when a
rule changes; preserve the earlier report's provenance rather than silently
rewriting its historical result.
