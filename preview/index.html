03 / HOW IT WORKS

A simple rhythm.
A more deliberate routine.

Your data has a home. Your decisions have context.

01

Record

Add income and expenses with categories, dates and payment details. Keep accounts and card bills in context.

02

Understand

Explore cash flow, category expenses and dated investment values. Ask Tuxedo to help interpret your records.

03

Plan

Set category limits, organize goals and compare monthly scenarios. Review assistant proposals before saving a change.
